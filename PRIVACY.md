# Privacy Policy for HA LiveKit

HA LiveKit connects Home Assistant with iPhone Live Activities.

## Data stored on your device

HA LiveKit may store your Home Assistant URL and Long-Lived Access Token on your device so the app can connect to your Home Assistant instance. These credentials are stored locally on your device and are not sold or used for advertising.

## Background Live Activities

To support background Live Activity updates, HA LiveKit may register Apple ActivityKit/APNs tokens with a managed relay service. These tokens are used only to deliver Live Activity updates to your device.

The relay may store technical identifiers required for app functionality, such as device/activity tokens and a Home Assistant instance identifier. These are used only to route Live Activity updates and are not used for advertising or tracking.

The managed relay does not receive or store your Home Assistant Long-Lived Access Token.

## Home Assistant data

HA LiveKit can read Home Assistant entity names, states, units, and progress values when you connect the app or use the Home Assistant integration. This information is used to display Live Activities and app UI.

## Diagnostics

HA LiveKit may show local diagnostics to help troubleshoot connection and background update issues. Diagnostics are designed to avoid exposing full tokens or secrets. You should still avoid sharing screenshots or logs that may contain private URLs or Home Assistant details.

## Data sharing

HA LiveKit does not sell user data. HA LiveKit does not use user data for third-party advertising or tracking.
